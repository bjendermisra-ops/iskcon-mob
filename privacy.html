Shree Shree Radha Krishna Chandra Mandir

How we collect, use and protect your information

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 Google Play Data Safety Compliant

Hare Krishna! Welcome to the official Shree Shree Radha Krishna Chandra Mandir mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard your information when you use our app, in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

Quick Summary

  • No login or account is needed to use the app.
  • We never store your card details or UPI PIN.
  • Camera and microphone audio/video is never recorded or stored.
  • We never sell your personal data to anyone.
  • You can request deletion of your data at any time.

1. Information We Collect

Most features of the app (Live Darshan, Kirtan, Daily Schedule, Stories and Games) do not require any personal data. We follow a "No Forced Login" model. We process information only in the following situations:

A. Donations & 80G Tax Exemption

When you voluntarily donate through the app, we may collect the following to issue your receipt, offer Prasadam and facilitate 80G tax exemption:

  • Full Name
  • Mobile Number & Email Address
  • PAN details (only if you opt for 80G tax exemption)

Note on Financial Data: We do not store your credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs. All payments are processed securely through regulated third-party payment apps and gateways (such as PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm or your bank app).

B. App Settings & Local Device Storage

To give you a smooth experience, some preferences are saved only on your own device. This data is NOT uploaded to our servers:

  • Japa Mala counter and daily rounds.
  • Game levels (locked/unlocked status).
  • Theme (Dark/Light) and language (Hindi/English).
  • Display name saved locally for quick joining of Live Japa sessions.
C. Event Registrations, Forms & Surveys

For temple festivals, seminars, youth or Sunday school programs and volunteer activities, we may use in-app forms or secure external links (like Google Forms). If you choose to participate, we may collect:

  • Full Name, Email Address and Phone/WhatsApp Number.
  • Event-related details (date, seva preference, age group, etc.).

We use this data only to organize the specific event and send you passes, links or schedule updates. We NEVER share, rent or sell it to marketing agencies or third parties.

2. Device Permissions

Our app requests only the minimum permissions needed to work properly:

Sensitive Permissions: Camera & Microphone

The "Live Global Japa" feature lets devotees chant interactively with the temple through an integrated web-conferencing tool (Zoom Web API). For this:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): used only to stream your live video during the session.
  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): used only to transmit your voice/chanting during the session.

We DO NOT record, store, save or share your audio or video. Data is processed in real time only for the live session. You can mute or turn off your camera at any time.

  • Internet & Network: to load Live Darshan, Kirtan media and the temple's dynamic schedule.
  • Foreground Service & Wake Lock: keeps your phone awake while you listen to continuous Kirtan or watch Live Darshan.
  • Google Play License Check: a standard Android check to confirm the app is installed from official channels.
  • Phone State: used only by our audio player to detect incoming calls and pause Kirtan automatically. We DO NOT read or store your call logs, phone number, contacts or device IMEI.
  • Location (Map Redirection): the "Temple Directory/Map" only opens third-party map apps. We do NOT track or store your GPS location.

3. External Links & Other Apps

The app contains links to our official online store (books, spiritual items and merchandise), other official apps of the temple, and social media channels (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram). It may also open third-party payment apps for donations.

Once you leave our app through such a link, your data and purchases are governed by the privacy policy and terms of that website, platform or app.

4. Third-Party Services & Ads

To keep the app running smoothly and cover server costs, we use trusted third-party services. They may collect device identifiers and usage data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase: app analytics, remote configuration, real-time database and crash prevention.
  • Blogger API & YouTube: to load images, gallery content and live video Darshan.
  • Zoom Web SDK / API: to run the "Live Global Japa" video sessions.
  • Ad Networks (Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Start.io): we show ads to support app maintenance. These networks may use your device's Advertising ID, IP address and app interaction data to show personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • AdID & AdServices (Privacy Sandbox): we use the Advertising ID permission, AdServices Topics and Attribution APIs, and the Play Install Referrer API for ad measurement without collecting sensitive personal information. You can reset or manage ad personalization anytime from your phone's Google settings.

5. Push Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send spiritual updates such as Ekadashi reminders, festival alerts and Live Darshan timings. You can turn notifications on or off anytime from your device settings.

6. Stories, Games & Quizzes

The app has spiritual stories, quizzes and games (memory match, puzzles) made for users of all ages. No personal data, login or tracking identifier is collected by these features; they work without any user information.

7. General Audience & Children

This app is built for a General Audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from any user (including children) to use the main features, stories or games. Information is collected only during voluntary donations or event registrations, which are meant for adults. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has submitted personal information without your consent, please contact us and we will remove it.

8. Feedback & Communication

You can contact the temple through WhatsApp or email from within the app. In that case your phone number or email address is visible to our team. We use it only to answer your query and never share or sell it.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

Because the app has no accounts, there is no user profile to delete. Personal data such as donation or event registration records is kept only as long as needed for legal, accounting, tax or event purposes. Audio and video from live sessions is never retained.

How to Request Data Deletion

To delete your donation records, event registration details or contact details, or to withdraw your consent, email us at devsantosh.tech@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". After verifying your request, we will permanently delete your personal data from our active records within 15-30 business days.

10. Data Security & Policy Updates

We use industry-standard security (HTTPS/SSL encryption) to protect your data in transit. However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new features or Google Play policy changes, and the updated date will be shown at the top. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
